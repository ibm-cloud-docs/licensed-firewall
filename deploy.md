## Deploying a licensed Fortinet firewall in the console
{: #deploy-licensed-fortinet-firewall-console}

Deploy a FortiGate PAYGO firewall from the IBM Cloud catalog.
{: shortdesc}

1. Go to the IBM Cloud catalog
1. Search for Fortinet FortiGate
1. Select the PAYGO offering
1. Choose a license tier (ATP, UTM, Enterprise)
1. Select a compute profile
1. Configure VPC, subnets, and interfaces
1. Review pricing
1. Click **Create**.

The firewall is now provisioned with licensing applied.
